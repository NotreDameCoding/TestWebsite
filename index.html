Welcome to Populatle 🌱

Each day brings five gentle rounds. You get a continent and a population.

  1. Draw a loop around a bundle of countries in that continent.
  2. Tap a country to add or remove it.
  3. Get your bundle's total as close to the target as you can.
  4. Each round is worth up to 1000 points.
